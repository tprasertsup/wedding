The Couple

Two worlds,

Bangkok met the Pacific Northwest, and everything made sense after that.

She carries the warmth of Thailand in her smile, and he carries the quiet steadiness of someone shaped by two cultures at once. Together, Nine and Tom have built a love that honors where they come from while making room for everything they are becoming. Their wedding in Nonthaburi is a celebration of that fusion — Thai tradition, Vietnamese American roots, and a future that belongs entirely to them.

Thai Khan Maak Procession & Tea Ceremony

The afternoon opens with the Khan Maak — a vibrant traditional Thai bridal procession, where gifts are carried to the bride's home accompanied by music, laughter, and ceremony. This is followed by the intimate and deeply meaningful Tea Ceremony, where Nine and Tom honour their families and receive blessings for their marriage.

Evening Reception & Dinner

As dusk settles over Nonthaburi, the celebration transforms into an elegant evening reception. Join Nine and Tom for a candlelit dinner, heartfelt toasts, dancing, and all the moments in between that make a wedding night unforgettable. Dress code is neutral, earthy, and celebratory.
