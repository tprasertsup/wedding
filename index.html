The Celebration

July 11, 2027  ·  Le Pansiri, Nonthaburi

15.30
Afternoon
Thai Khan Maak Procession
& Tea Ceremony

The afternoon begins with the Khan Maak — a joyful, music-filled Thai bridal procession in which the groom and his party carry beautifully arranged ceremonial gifts and offerings to the bride's family, symbolising respect, abundance, and the formal request for her hand. Elders offer their blessings during the sacred sai monkhon thread ceremony, a looped cord of white thread that crowns the couple and joins them in a single, unbroken circle of love. The water-pouring blessing follows, with family and guests gently pouring lustral water over the couple's hands as a wish for a life of goodness and grace. The afternoon closes with the tea ceremony — Nine and Tom kneel before their families, offering tea as a gesture of deep honour and gratitude to the elders who shaped them.

18.00
Evening
Evening Reception
& Dinner

As the golden hour fades over Nonthaburi, the celebration opens into an elegant evening of candlelight, cuisine, and pure joy. Begin with cocktails and the warmth of reunion — friends and family from Thailand, the United States, and beyond, all gathered in one room for the first time. A long, beautiful dinner follows, with toasts from those who know Nine and Tom best, words that will make the room laugh and cry in equal measure. Music fills the night as the dance floor opens, and two families — one from Bangkok, one from Washington — find their way to each other, step by step, until it feels like they were always one.
