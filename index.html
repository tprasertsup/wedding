The Celebration

July 11, 2027  ·  Le Pansiri, Nonthaburi

15.30 Afternoon
🪷
Khan Maak Procession
The groom leads a joyful, music-filled procession bearing ceremonial gifts to the bride's family — a festive, formal request for her hand.
🚪
Gate Games
Friends of the bride playfully block the way, setting fun challenges for the groom and his groomsmen before granting entry.
🧵
Sai Monkhon Ceremony
A sacred looped thread crowns Nine and Tom together, joining two lives in an unbroken circle of love and blessing.
💧
Water Blessing
Family and guests gently pour lustral water over the couple's hands, wishing them a life of goodness and grace.
🍵
Tea Ceremony
Nine and Tom kneel before their families, offering tea as a gesture of deep honour and gratitude to the elders who shaped them.
18.00 Evening
🥂
Reception & Reunion
Photos, laughter, and long-awaited hugs as two families finally meet and friends from across the world find each other again.
🍽️
Dinner & Toasts
A long, beautiful dinner with speeches from those who know Nine and Tom best — words that will make you laugh and cry in equal measure.
💃
After Party
No formalities — just good music, good drinks, and everyone letting loose. The night goes as long as you want it to.
