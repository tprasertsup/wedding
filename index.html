How the day runs

The Celebration

July 11, 2027  ·  Le Pansiri, Nonthaburi

Afternoon 15.30

🪷Khan Maak Procession

The groom leads a joyful, music-filled procession bearing ceremonial gifts to the bride's family. At the door, her friends block the way with playful gate games before letting him through.

🍵Tea Ceremony

Nine and Tom kneel before their families, offering tea as a gesture of deep honor and gratitude to the elders who raised them.

💧Sai Monkhon & Water Blessing

A sacred looped thread crowns Nine and Tom together, joining two lives in one unbroken circle. Family and guests then pour lustral water over their hands, wishing them a life of goodness and grace.

Evening 18.00

🥂Reception & Reunion

Photos, laughter, and long-awaited hugs as two families finally meet and friends from across the world find each other again.

🍽️Dinner & Toasts

A long, beautiful dinner with speeches from those who know Nine and Tom best — words that will make you laugh and cry in equal measure.

💃After Party

No formalities — just good music, good drinks, and everyone letting loose. The night goes as long as you want it to.
