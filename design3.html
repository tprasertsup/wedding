The Day

Ceremony Program

A day that weaves Thai tradition with pure celebration — two ceremonies, one beautiful love story.

15:30

Afternoon Ceremony

Thai Khan Maak Procession & Tea Ceremony

  • Khan Maak Procession — The groom's family arrives bearing ceremonial gifts through a joyful, music-filled parade.
  • Blessing by Elders — Respected elders bestow blessings and tie sacred threads (sai monkhon) around the couple's wrists.
  • Water Pouring Ceremony — Guests pour lustral water over the couple's joined hands as a blessing for a harmonious life.
  • Tea Ceremony — Honoring both Thai and Vietnamese heritage, the couple serve tea to family elders as an act of gratitude.

❀ Traditional Thai dress welcome and encouraged

18:00

Evening Celebration

Evening Reception & Dinner

  • Cocktail Hour — Welcome drinks and canapés as the sun sets over the gardens of Le Pansiri.
  • Grand Entrance — The newlyweds make their debut as husband and wife for the very first time.
  • Banquet Dinner — A curated menu celebrating Thai and Vietnamese flavors, lovingly prepared for the occasion.
  • Toasts, Dancing & Festivities — An evening of music, laughter, and memories that will last a lifetime.
