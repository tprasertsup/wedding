# Day-of Timing — Internal Notes

Not published. `docs/` is excluded from the deploy in `.vercelignore`, so
nothing here reaches guests. This is the place for timing we are still
firming up, or that we have decided deliberately **not** to put on the site.

---

## What the site currently tells guests

| Where | What it says |
|---|---|
| Venue → Time | Ceremony from 15:30 · Reception from 18:00 · Ends 21:00 |
| Programme → Afternoon | 15.30 |
| Programme → Evening | 18.00 (no close time — it lives in the venue block only) |
| Add to Google Calendar | 18:00–21:00 Bangkok (`20270711T110000Z/20270711T140000Z`) |

---

## Known, deliberately not on the site

- **The afternoon may finish early.** It could wrap around 17:00, or run
  through to 18:00. We are not publishing an afternoon end time, so there is
  nothing to correct if it moves.
- **Guests may arrive from 17:00, not only 18:00.** The evening programme
  still starts around 18:00. The site says "Reception from 18:00" because
  that is when things actually begin; anyone arriving at 17:00 is welcome but
  would be waiting.

Worth revisiting closer to the day:

- If the gap between the afternoon ending and 18:00 turns out to be long and
  guests are staying on site through it, they may need to be told what
  happens in between (drinks? somewhere to sit?) — an hour with no
  information is the kind of thing people remember.
- If we want the 17:00 arrival to be a real option rather than a tolerated
  one, that is a sentence on the site, not a note here.

## Open

- The 15:30 ceremony falls outside the calendar entry, which covers
  18:00–21:00. Correct if the afternoon is family and close friends only. If
  every guest is invited to both, the calendar wants either a 15:30–21:00
  span or two separate entries.
- Whether 21:00 is the venue's hard turn-out or just when our programme
  ends. The site currently reads as "everyone out at 21:00".
- The after party is off the public programme on purpose — it is a smaller
  group, away from the venue. Those guests need telling some other way.
