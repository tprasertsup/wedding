Ceremony Program

July 11, 2027  ·  Le Pansiri, Nonthaburi

15:30
Thai Khan Maak Procession & Tea Ceremony

Guests are invited to witness the sacred Khan Maak procession — a joyful parade bearing gifts and offerings to honour the union. Following the procession, join us for the intimate Thai Tea Ceremony, where Nine and Tom pay respect to family elders and receive their blessings for a prosperous life together.

🌺
✨
18:00
Evening Reception & Dinner

As the sun sets over Nonthaburi, celebrate the newlyweds with an elegant evening of fine dining, heartfelt toasts, and dancing. A night filled with warmth, laughter, and the joy of two families becoming one. Dinner service, speeches, and celebrations throughout the evening.
